Project Site Plan

Site Name - Gear & Ride

I chose the name Gear & Ride because it reflects the two main aspects of my website: motorcycle equipment and the riding experience.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Gear & Ride is to create an online space where motorcycle lovers can:

  • Connect with other riders and go on group tours
  • Learn from professional instructors through classes and tutorials
  • Buy high-quality gear like jackets, gloves, and helmets
  • Discover different motorcycle models and compare them

I want this site to be helpful for beginners and experienced riders alike, offering real value and a strong community feel.

Scenarios

1. Where can I sign up for a group motorcycle tour nearby?

2. What kind of riding gear should I buy for long-distance touring?

3. How do I book lessons with a certified riding instructor?

Color Schema

Primary Color #1f1f1f - Header, footer, and nav bar background

Accent Color #ff5e00 - CTA buttons, links, and highlights

Background Base #f4f4f4 - Main content background

Secondary Text #444444 - Body text, descriptions

Typography
